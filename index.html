01 Campaign Summary

Aviator Gear is running a two-week Design Your Freedom Patch contest. Followers submit an entry in one of two ways.

  • Design from scratch. Create a new Freedom-themed patch concept.
  • Convert an existing patch. Send in the unit patch they already have. Aviator Gear adapts it into a Freedom Patch concept for them.

The "you don't have to start from zero" angle is the strategic core of this campaign. With only two reels, both need to make both entry paths unmistakable.

Prize · What They're Winning

50 free patches of the winning design, produced by Aviator Gear.

02 Entry Mechanics

Surface these in on-screen text and CTAs. Don't rephrase between the two reels. Repetition drives clarity.

  • Instagram. Post your design or existing patch to your story and tag @aviatorgear.
  • Facebook. Comment your design or existing patch on the contest post.
  • TikTok. Post your design or existing patch, tag @aviatorgear, and use #DesignYourFreedomPatch.

03 Audience · Tone · Role of Video

Audience. Aviator Gear's existing community. Pilots, veterans, active military, aviation enthusiasts, unit members, crew families. They respond to craft, heritage, and authenticity. They do not respond to cheesy patriotic hype or contest-y hard sells.

Role of video. With two weeks and two reels, these have to do heavy lifting. Reel 1 launches the campaign and plants both entry paths. Reel 2 drives the final push before deadline.

Tone. Confident, proud, community-oriented. Tasteful patriotism, not jingoistic, not Fourth-of-July-ad energy. Restraint over loudness. Think: a pilot talking to another pilot, not a marketer shouting.

Do Not
  • Do not reference the "20% more" promotion. That's paid messaging and is not part of the organic campaign.
  • Do not overplay red, white, and blue iconography. Avoid generic Fourth-of-July stock vibe.
  • Do not make it feel like a giveaway. This is a craft-forward design contest.

04 Shared Production Notes

  • Subtitle all spoken or VO content. 80%+ view with sound off.
  • Consistent Aviator Gear logo end card across both reels.
  • Consistent font family and type treatment. One visual language.
  • Music: library and licensed tracks only. Avoid viral TikTok audio that gets flagged on IG.
  • Deliver Reel 1 in three cuts: 15s, 22s, and 30s for platform testing.
  • Final files: 9:16 MP4 (H.264, 1080x1920) plus captioned SRT.

05 The Two Reels

Reel 01

Contest Launch

Delivery · May 1 Duration: 15 to 25s (15 / 22 / 30s cuts)
Objective
Launch the campaign. Make both entry paths clear inside the first 15 seconds. With only one other reel to back this up, this one has to carry weight.
Takeaway
"Aviator Gear is running a Freedom Patch contest. I can either design one or have them adapt my existing one."
Hook Options
"Design your own Freedom Patch. Or let us adapt yours." "You don't have to start from zero."
Concept
Fast, confident opening. Tactile craft shots. Hard pivot to the two entry paths. Land on prize and deadline.
Shot List
  1. Close-up: patch being stitched (macro, satisfying texture).
  2. Hands holding an existing unit patch, close-up on the detail.
  3. Pen sketching a new design on paper.
  4. Aviator Gear product in motion (flight jacket, patches visible).
  5. American flag detail, restrained, not overplayed.
  6. Close-up: finished Freedom-themed patch on a garment.
  7. Stack of patches, a quantity shot that pays off the 50-patch prize.
On-Screen Text
0 to 2sDESIGN YOUR FREEDOM PATCH
3 to 6sDesign from scratch.
7 to 10sOr send us your unit patch.
11 to 13sWe'll adapt it.
14 to 17sWinner gets 50 free patches.
18 to 21sDeadline: May 15.
End card@aviatorgear · #DesignYourFreedomPatch
Voiceover (optional)
Two ways to enter. Design your own Freedom Patch, or send us the one you've already got, and we'll work it into something new. Winner takes home 50.
CTA
Tag @aviatorgear with your entry.
Pacing / Music
Upbeat but restrained. Drumline, cinematic, or modern Americana. Not hype EDM.
Reel 02

Deadline · Final Push

Delivery · May 11 Duration: 15 to 20s
Objective
Final push. Land the remaining submissions before the May 15 deadline. Keep both entry paths visible so anyone who hasn't submitted still sees the adapt-your-existing-patch angle.
Takeaway
"Deadline's Friday. If I'm entering, from scratch or from my existing patch, it's now."
Hook Options
"Four days left." "Still got a patch sitting in your notes app? Send it."
Concept
Tight and urgent. Open with a clock or countdown cue. Hit both entry paths, the prize, and the deadline. End on the submission call.
Shot List
  1. Ticking clock or sand timer (stock).
  2. Hands holding an existing unit patch (reinforces the adaptation path).
  3. Sketch being finalized on paper or tablet.
  4. Stack of patches, prize visual.
  5. Final patch shot, payoff.
  6. Metaphorical "submission" moment (envelope closing, upload screen, hand tapping post).
