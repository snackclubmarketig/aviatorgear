01 Campaign Summary

Aviator Gear is running the Design Your Freedom Patch contest through May. Followers submit an entry in one of two ways.

  • Design from scratch. Create a new Freedom-themed patch concept.
  • Convert an existing patch. Send in the unit patch they already have. Aviator Gear adapts it into a Freedom Patch concept for them.

The "you don't have to start from zero" angle is the strategic core of this campaign. Every reel should make it clear that both entry paths exist.

Prize · What They're Winning

50 free patches of the winning design, produced by Aviator Gear.

02 Entry Mechanics

These should surface in on-screen text and CTAs. Don't rephrase across reels. Repetition drives clarity.

  • Instagram. Post your design or existing patch to your story and tag @aviatorgear.
  • Facebook. Comment your design or existing patch on the contest post.
  • TikTok. Post your design or existing patch, tag @aviatorgear, and use #DesignYourFreedomPatch.

03 Audience · Tone · Role of Video

Audience. Aviator Gear's existing community. Pilots, veterans, active military, aviation enthusiasts, unit members, crew families. They respond to craft, heritage, and authenticity. They do not respond to cheesy patriotic hype or contest-y hard sells.

Role of video. Reels drive attention and emotional pull. Statics and carousels handle the details. The reels need to make people stop, feel the campaign, and act.

Tone. Confident, proud, community-oriented. Tasteful patriotism, not jingoistic, not Fourth-of-July-ad energy. Restraint over loudness. Think: a pilot talking to another pilot, not a marketer shouting.

Do Not
  • Do not reference the "20% more" promotion. That's paid messaging and is not part of the organic campaign.
  • Do not overplay red, white, and blue iconography. Avoid generic Fourth-of-July stock vibe.
  • Do not make it feel like a giveaway. This is a craft-forward design contest.

04 Shared Production Notes

  • Subtitle all spoken or VO content. 80%+ view with sound off.
  • Consistent Aviator Gear logo end card across all four reels.
  • Consistent font family and type treatment across the set. One visual language.
  • Music: library and licensed tracks only. Avoid viral TikTok audio that gets flagged on IG.
  • Deliver Reel 1 in three cuts: 15s, 22s, and 30s for platform testing.
  • Final files: 9:16 MP4 (H.264, 1080x1920) plus captioned SRT.

Reel 01

Contest Launch

Delivery · May 1 Duration: 15 to 25s (15 / 22 / 30s cuts)

Launch the campaign. Make both entry paths clear inside the first 15 seconds.
Takeaway
"Aviator Gear is running a Freedom Patch contest. I can either design one from scratch or have them adapt my existing one."

"Design your own Freedom Patch. Or let us adapt yours." "You don't have to start from zero."

  2. Hands holding an existing unit patch, close-up on the detail.
  3. Pen sketching a new design on paper.
  4. Aviator Gear product in motion (flight jacket, patches visible).
  5. American flag detail, restrained, not overplayed.
  6. Close-up: finished Freedom-themed patch on a garment.
  7. Stack of patches, a quantity shot that pays off the 50-patch prize.
