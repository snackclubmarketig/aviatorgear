01 Campaign Summary

Aviator Gear is running the Design Your Freedom Patch contest through May. Followers submit an entry in one of two ways.

  • Design from scratch. Create a new Freedom-themed patch concept.
  • Convert an existing patch. Send in the unit patch they already have. Aviator Gear adapts it into a Freedom Patch concept for them.

The "you don't have to start from zero" angle is the strategic core of this campaign. Both entry paths should be visible in every post.

Prize · What They're Winning

50 free patches of the winning design, produced by Aviator Gear.

02 Entry Mechanics

Use the exact same mechanics language across every post. Repetition drives clarity. Do not rephrase.

  • Instagram. Post your design or existing patch to your story and tag @aviatorgear.
  • Facebook. Comment your design or existing patch on the contest post.
  • TikTok. Post your design or existing patch, tag @aviatorgear, and use #DesignYourFreedomPatch.

04 Rules & Guardrails

Do Not
  • Do not reference the "20% more" promotion. That's paid messaging and is not part of the organic campaign.
  • Do not overplay red, white, and blue iconography. Tasteful patriotism, not Fourth-of-July-ad energy.
  • Do not soften the "you don't have to start from scratch" angle. That's the strategic core.
  • Do not rephrase the entry mechanics across posts. Repetition is the point.
  • Use #DesignYourFreedomPatch consistently in every caption.
  • Tone: confident, proud, community-oriented. Restraint over loudness.
  • Start collecting submission screenshots from May 2 so there's a full grid ready for Carousel 4 (Last Chance) and Week 5 spotlight.

10 Production Notes for the VA

  • Build all on-image copy in Canva using the provided FB, IG, and Story templates. Apply each headline, support line, and CTA to the corresponding template slot.
  • If a template has less room than the full on-image copy, keep the headline + CTA and move the support line into the caption.
  • Story versions should always include a "Tap for details" sticker linking to the feed post.
  • Use the same Aviator Gear logo lockup and color treatment across all six weeks. One campaign, one visual system.
  • Start screenshotting tagged submissions on May 2 so Carousel 04 (Last Chance) and Week 5 Winner Spotlight have a full visual bank to pull from.
  • Questions on tone, phrasing, or platform fit go to the campaign lead before publishing. Do not freestyle captions.
